# Activity coverage

The supplied curriculum order is retained. Primary conventions come from Nicoleta Daniela Sârbu, *Geometrie descriptivă și desen tehnic* (2017). Mathematical elaboration and exceptions follow the supplied extended academic support. The 38 existing seminar exercises and their 14 reference illustrations are preserved separately in the integrated application.

| Unit | Interactive activities | Student construction and verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Orthogonal, central and parallel oblique projection | H/V/W point images; ray-plane intersection; central magnification and point-at-infinity case |
| 2 | Complete epure; reflection in H | Manual n, n′ and n″; four diedra, projection planes, OX and both bisectors; reflected point |
| 3 | General line; profile line | Endpoint projections, projected lines, H/V/W traces; parallel, contained and coincident-point cases |
| 4 | General plane traces; line-plane piercing; plane-plane intersection | Two points on each trace and the trace lines; K/K′; intersection of two planes with parallel H/V traces |
| 5 | Plane rabatment; rotation around OZ; vertical-plane change | Rotated points, preserved radii/elevation, new local reference frame; animated rigid transformations |
| 6 | Oblique prism; pyramid level section | Correlated projections of vertices and a diagonal; depth-based 3D occlusion; explicit visibility question; section polygon |
| 7 | Sphere and cone level sections | Section endpoints and radius; tangent/exterior sphere and finite-cone exceptions |
| 8 | Oblique cylinder and cone sections | Generator/plane intersections; finite-surface clipping; ellipse semiaxis; conic classification |
| 9 | Cylinder and cone development | Rectangle boundary and lateral area; sector generators, circular arc and sector angle; animated surface development |
| 10 | Scale construction and correlated views | Paper rectangle in mm, real dimension text independent of scale, H/V/W vertices and diagonal |

All activities use generated coordinates and equations. The reference laboratory remains available in each unit for comparison with the supplied validated figures. The new construction board does not digitize or automatically grade arbitrary photographed handwritten drawings; it validates the point/line/arc construction entered in the app.
